Why rotate a PDF?

A page scanned sideways or upside down is annoying to read and looks unprofessional when shared. Rotating inside a PDF viewer only changes how it looks on your screen — the file itself stays wrong for everyone else. iWorkPDF writes the new orientation directly into the file, so it's fixed permanently.

FAQ

Is the rotation saved permanently?

Yes. Unlike rotating in a viewer, the new orientation is written into the exported PDF and stays that way everywhere.

Can I rotate only some pages?

Yes, you can rotate all pages at once or select specific pages to rotate individually.
