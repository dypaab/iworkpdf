How to Delete Pages from a PDF for Free

Remove blank, duplicate or unwanted pages from a PDF — directly in your browser. No upload, no account.

Why delete pages from a PDF?

Scanned documents often contain blank pages, duplicate scans, or a cover sheet you don't need. Removing them makes the file cleaner, smaller and easier to share — without re-creating the whole document.

Step-by-step guide

1

Open your PDF

Drop your PDF into the Delete Pages tool to preview every page.

2

Select the pages to remove

Type page numbers or ranges (e.g. 1, 3, 5-8), or click pages in the preview.

3

Download the trimmed PDF

Export the new PDF without the removed pages, instantly.

FAQ

Are bookmarks kept after deleting pages?

Yes. The Delete Pages tool removes only the pages you select and preserves the rest of the document, including bookmarks and internal links where possible.

Can I remove several pages at once?

Yes, select any combination of pages (e.g. 1, 3, 5-8) and remove them in a single step.
