How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF for Free

Add clean page numbers to any PDF — directly in your browser. No upload, no account, no watermark.

🔢 Use the tool — free

Why add page numbers?

Numbered pages make a report, contract or dissertation easier to navigate, cite and print in the right order. Adding them afterwards saves you from re-exporting the original document.

Step-by-step guide

1

Open your PDF

Drop your PDF into the Page Numbers tool.

2

Pick the position

Choose where the number appears — for example bottom-center or bottom-right.

3

Download the numbered PDF

The numbers are drawn onto each page and the PDF is downloaded instantly.

FAQ

Can I choose where the numbers appear?

Yes, you can choose the position (for example bottom-center or bottom-right) so the numbers fit your document.

Does it change the rest of the PDF?

No. Only the page numbers are added on top; the original content is left untouched.
